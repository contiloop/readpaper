# Project Goal

## Why This Project Exists

Commercial AI assistants often fail not only by skipping parts of a paper, but also by misunderstanding its content. They may produce fluent and confident explanations that distort definitions, methods, evidence, or conclusions. This makes it difficult for the user to determine whether their own understanding is wrong or the AI has misread the paper. ReadPaper exists to reduce and expose these failures through complete reading, repeated cross-checking, and direct grounding in the original source.

Q&A, criticism, and explanation do not need separate prebuilt pipelines or databases. Once the agent has fully read the paper and remains grounded in the complete source, ordinary user prompts can request those tasks naturally.

## Core Purpose

> **Complete reading + deep paper-grounded interaction**

## Precise Objective

Given a paper link, make the active AI agent completely read the paper and enter a source-grounded working state in which it can interact deeply with the paper.

## Deep Paper-Grounded Interaction

Deep paper-grounded interaction means:

- The complete paper remains accessible.
- The agent connects information across sections.
- It revisits exact pages, figures, tables, and equations when needed.
- It distinguishes the paper's claims from its own inference.
- Subsequent work remains grounded in original evidence.
